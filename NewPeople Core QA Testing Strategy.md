### Planner Response

In simple terms, **People Core is the digital foundation for managing employee information in a wellness-focused HR system**.

Think of it like this:

## The Problem It Solves
Small and medium businesses struggle with scattered employee data across spreadsheets, different systems, and paper files. This makes it hard to:
- Keep track of who works for the company
- Manage employee information consistently
- Support employee wellness programs
- Make HR decisions based on reliable data

## What People Core Does
People Core creates a **single, reliable source of truth** for all employee information:

1. **Basic Employee Records** - Names, contact info, hire dates, job details
2. **Employment History** - Contracts, promotions, status changes over time
3. **Wellness Integration** - Connects employee data to wellness programs and health engagement
4. **Multi-Company Support** - Can serve multiple businesses while keeping their data completely separate

## The "Wellness" Focus
Unlike traditional HR systems that just track payroll and benefits, People Core is designed specifically to support employee wellness and health engagement. It helps companies:
- Get employee consent for wellness programs
- Track wellness participation
- Connect with health systems (like Zimasa Health Engagement Platform)

## The Foundation
People Core is the **foundation** that other HR modules build on top of:
- **Work Lattice** (org charts, positions, reporting)
- **Leave Management** (vacation, sick days)
- **Performance Management** (reviews, goals)
- **Gamification** (wellness challenges, rewards)

So in layman's terms: **It's the central database that knows everything about each employee, designed specifically to help companies take better care of their people's health and wellness.**

## QA Testing Approach for People Core

### **1. Database Layer Testing (Foundation)**

- **Multi-Tenancy Safety**: Ensure zero cross-tenant data leakage at database level
- **Audit Trail Testing**: Every data change is tracked with created_at, updated_at, etc.
- **Soft Delete Testing**: Data is marked as deleted but not actually removed

### **2. Business Logic Testing (Core Functionality)**
Testing the actual People Core features:

- **Person Management**: Create, update, search people across tenants
- **Employee Management**: Hire, status changes, contract management
- **Data Relationships**: Person → Employee → Contract → Status History
- **Business Rules**: Employee numbers unique within tenant, status transitions

### **3. API Layer Testing (Interface)**
Testing the REST APIs that developers will use:

- **CRUD Operations**: Create, read, update, delete for all entities
- **Multi-Tenancy Security**: Tenant A cannot access Tenant B data
- **Error Handling**: Proper error codes and messages
- **Performance**: Response times under load

### **4. Integration Testing (Critical Flows)**
Testing end-to-end business scenarios:

- **Employee Onboarding**: Person → Employee → Contract → Contacts
- **Status Changes**: Active → Probation → Active with full history tracking
- **Cross-Domain**: People Core connecting to Work Lattice (positions)

### **5. Performance & Security Testing**
Ensuring the system is production-ready:

- **Database Performance**: Queries execute < 100ms with proper indexing

Every data change must be tracked:
